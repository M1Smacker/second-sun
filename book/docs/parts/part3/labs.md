# Try it yourself: Part 3

<p class="ss-meta">Optional · hands-on · at your PC</p>

!!! plain "In plain words"
    You can read the whole book without these. Each lab shows the real output first, so you can follow along without running anything. The commands are in a fold, for when you are at your PC.

## [Lab 4: find a planet's dip](../../labs/lab4-find-a-dip.md)

<p class="ss-meta">Lab · at your PC · about 20 min · Level L3 · after Chapters 7 and 8</p>

You look at one month of TOI-1338 data, the system with a known two-star planet. First you try to spot the planet's dip by eye. Then you run our real search on it and see whether it agrees with you. This is the core move of the whole hunt.

## [Lab 6: hide your own fake planets](../../labs/lab6-fake-planets.md)

<p class="ss-meta">Lab · at your PC · about 20 min (the script runs 3 to 5 minutes) · Level L5 · after Chapter 9</p>

You hide 20 fake planets (5 sizes, 4 places each) in real TOI-1338 data and run our search on every one. The table shows which were found, and how much of each dip survived our cleaning. You've just measured what our search can see.
