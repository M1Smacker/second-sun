## In plain words (read this first)

**Submitting a candidate (CTOI) works for single dips too.** By mid-2021, **168** official TESS candidates had started life as community submissions, *including single transits* that NASA's automatic searches had thrown out, and fainter stars that MIT's pipeline skipped (K1). That's exactly our kind of find. The exact upload form is behind an ExoFOP login, so its fields are still unknown; we'll see them when you create the ExoFOP account.

**Publishing can be free.** The AAS's short-paper journal, **Research Notes of the AAS (RNAAS), charges nothing** (K3). Full papers in AJ/ApJ cost **$1,425 and up**, with no waiver listed (K2). Our realistic path:
- **RNAAS** for our own short results: a method note, a candidate note, a "TESS caught TOI-1338 b again" note.
- **Co-authorship** on a professional team's full paper for a big discovery (they pay the fee, as usual).

**AI rules are now explicit (posted 9 Sept 2026 by the AAS):**
- **AI cannot be an author.** That matches our plan: Ahmed is the author.
- AI use must be **disclosed in a statement at the end of the article**, naming the tool and what it did.
- Figures made with AI need it in the caption.
- The rules cover *writing the paper*; they make **no rule against AI for code or analysis** (K4).

**arXiv** (where astronomers post papers) got stricter in 2026: a new author now needs an **endorsement** from someone who already publishes there (K5). For you, that most likely comes through a collaborator, another reason the "hand it to a pro team" route matters.

**AAVSO VSX (new variable stars, our side wins):** you register for VSX, check the catalogues yourself first, then submit. Credit goes with the submission (K6).

## 1. CTOI (Community TOI)
- Submitted to ExoFOP-TESS; the TESS TOI team reviews it and may assign a TOI number (MIT TESS site via search; K1).
- Name format: TIC ID + two-digit object number (e.g. TIC 260128333.01) (K1).
- **Single transits are accepted and have been promoted.** The promoted CTOIs include FFI targets fainter than QLP's vetting cut (T = 10.5) and single transits rejected by the QLP and SPOC periodic searches (Mireles+ 2021 TSC2 poster, zenodo 5130660; K1).
- **Unknown:** the exact upload fields (period for single transits? epoch, depth, duration, notes?). Resolve when the ExoFOP account exists.

## 3. AI-use policy (AAS, posted 2026-09-09; K4)
- "AI cannot be listed as an author on publications."
- A disclosure statement at the end of the article must say which tool was used and what for. Figures: caption names the tool (and the prompt if possible).
- The guidelines "concern the manuscript, not the data analysis": no recommendation on AI-written code or analysis. AI may refine or format code in the manuscript or supplements if disclosed.
- **Our template line (draft):** "Pipeline code, data retrieval and preliminary analysis were developed with Claude Code (Anthropic; Claude Opus 5.5) under the direction of the author; all candidates were inspected and approved by the author."

## 4. arXiv endorsement (K5)
- From **21 January 2026**, an institutional email alone no longer qualifies a new author; endorsement is per subject domain (arXiv blog, official).
- For an independent author, the likely route is an existing astro-ph author endorsing you (inferred; LIKELY).
