- 11:44 am. Chosen as the next lever while the fresh bench generates: it is the only untested design from F4 that needs no new light-curve work, only a scoring change.
- Rule: F4 section 3, fixed there at 11:05 am before any vouching number. Two deviations, both stricter, forced by the saved event lists (they hold only SNR 7+ events of the primary product): the vouching event must be SNR 7+ (F4 said 5+), and it must itself be tier 1 or held only by soft flags (F4 said only "not a common moment, not a mirror match"). Rule (iv) of F4 (crossing length against the binary's geometry) is NOT applied. Promoted event: held only by soft flags (dump or gap, ramp, dominated), depth cap respected. Pair test: same star, same arm, at least 3.5 binary periods apart, not within 1 hour of a whole number of binary periods, depths within a factor 2.
- Pass rule (F4, unchanged): promoted upside-down events in the whole untouched background at most 2; promoted real at most 2.3 x that plus 5; planets: paired change at least +1.0 point with interval above zero; then the fresh bench. Scored on top of B + depth cap and on top of B + depth cap + R2.
- What would prove it useless: by arithmetic before the run, about 225 real and 100 upside-down events are tier 1 or soft-held over about 650 stars, so chance pairs on one star are expected on a few per cent of stars. If promoted upside-down events exceed 2, vouching fails as designed.
