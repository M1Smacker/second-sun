## Independent measurements

`018-facts.py` imports NumPy, Astropy and SciPy, with no project pipeline or plotting imports. It reads finite, zero-quality-flag QLP SAP and SPOC PDCSAP measurements. Its dictionary-based time bins separately implement the stated averages. Eclipse middles come from the endpoints below 90% of each sector's median. A linear fit across independently counted cycles checks the period.

For the planet plots, it independently implements the local median-filter continuum and the final plotted-window median normalization. That last normalization matters for reproducing the quoted depths. A separate linear-continuum calculation checks sensitivity. Agreement with this processing recipe establishes reproducibility. It does not establish an unbiased physical transit fit or independent-noise significance.

The script counted 5 primary and 4 secondary eclipses and placed both predicted missing eclipses inside real gaps. It ran 47 arithmetic calculations, retained worked-example intermediate values, and compared all 17 new problem answers with independently calculated results. All 17 comparisons matched the source's stated answer tolerance.

These contradict the unconditional geometry and flat-bottom wording. Equations 14 to 19 support the duration calculation, and section 4.2 gives the photon-count exposure-time scaling.
