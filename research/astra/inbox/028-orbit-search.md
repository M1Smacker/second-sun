## Why
A circumbinary planet crosses its stars several times, at times that are NOT strictly periodic (the stars move, so crossing times and durations shift from one crossing to the next). Today Petrova judges each crossing alone. RS-037 (`research/stage3/rs037/MULTI-CROSSING-2026-11-15.md`) measured the ceiling with a crude equal-weight sum at the planted times: 3.9 points of P if a stack must reach SNR 7, 1.7 at SNR 9. Fable's X2 (`research/fable-method/X2-F4-PROTOTYPE.md`) tried "orbit vouching" with PAIRS of events and it failed badly (+1.9 points for 16 upside-down and 52 real new alarms): two strong events on one star are common by chance, and any two times fit some orbit. X2's own conclusion: three or more events whose times must fit ONE circumbinary orbit has real power. This job builds that properly and measures it, including the false-alarm price over ALL trial orbits a search would try (the thing RS-037 did not measure: its 0.15% null rate was only at the true planet times).

## What to build (design choices are yours; write them BEFORE scoring)

2. **Two uses, scored separately:** (A) promotion: a soft-held event (held only by dump or gap edge, ramp, dominated; depth cap respected) is promoted to tier 1 when it belongs to such a set; (B) stacking: a set whose combined score reaches a floor is a new tier-1 detection even if no single crossing reached SNR 7.
