# Census builder C1 and C2: can Gaia eclipsing-binary rows serve as the mask's ephemeris? (Claude, 23 Nov 2026, 10:47 am)

**Plain line: no. A Gaia eclipsing-binary row tells us THAT a star is an eclipsing binary; its period and eclipse time are too often wrong for binaries over 5 days. Census must take WHO from Gaia (DR4 on 2 Dec) and WHEN from our own fit to TESS light.**

Scripts: `c1_convention_check.py`, `c2_ttc_check.py` (this folder). Inputs: Gaia DR3 slow EB pool (188,407 rows, P over 5 d, `data/ffi_door_2026-11-08/gaia_pool_p5.csv`), Prsa 2022 TESS EB table (`data/xmatch/ebs.csv`), TESS Ten Thousand Catalog tables 3 and 4 (`data/g3/catalog_ttc`). Match: Gaia moved to J2000 by proper motion, 3 arcsec.

## What it changes
1. Census list = Gaia DR4 `vari_eclipsing_binary` (and the TESS lists) as the pointer to WHICH stars; period, epoch and eclipse widths from our own TESS fit, never from Gaia alone. The spec note's columns stay; `source` records both.
2. Our own TESS ephemeris fit is therefore on the critical path of Census AND of the mask timing pool (FIX 94/101, up to 5.3 points of P). Codex 012 failed its controls mostly on the half-period alias (RS-030: on the right period 38 of 45 agree within 0.001 d). Next build: alias resolution (odd/even eclipse depths and secondary phase), tested on the 012 controls.
