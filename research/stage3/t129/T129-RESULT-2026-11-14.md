# T129 result: events cluster on stars; the Re-search 1 verdicts stand, the power line was about 20% too generous (14 Nov 2026, night, Claude)

**Plain verdict: counting events as independent did not create or hide an excess (both lists stay NO EXCESS), but it made the nulls sound stronger than they are. With stars as the unit, the excess Re-search 1 could have seen is about 3.3 times expected on G2 (not 2.7) and about 6.0 times on G3 (not 5.2).**

Question (BUILD T129, from RS-007): do our null tests treat clustered real and upside-down events as independent? Answer: the Re-search 1 S1 test does (hypergeometric per bin on event counts, `pipeline/r1_readout.py` strat_block and the Fisher power line). `pipeline/scorecard_plus.py` already printed a design effect for the G2 sealed run; the Re-search 1 readout never did.

Method: `research/stage3/t129/t129_neff_r1.py`, counts only (fence B allows re-deriving the Re-search 1 counts; no search, no event time). Same selection as the readout (primary, recovered light, tier 1, PDCSAP half). Design effect deff = variance of the real share under a star-cluster bootstrap (20,000 draws) over the binomial variance; N_eff = events / deff. Power recomputed with counts scaled by deff. Cross-check: the event-level power from this script equals the readout's (G2 2.72 against 2.7 printed on 11 Nov; G3 5.2 against 5.2), so the selection is the same. CONFIRMED.
