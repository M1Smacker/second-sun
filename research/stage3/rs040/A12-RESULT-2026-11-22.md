# RS-040 A12 result: the field's detrending window is not better than the notch, and the notch's 18 Nov gain was overstated (Claude, 22 Nov 2026, 10:50 pm)

**Plain line: found something useful, a correction. A per-box window of 3 x the box length (the wotan rule) does NOT beat the notch at equal alarms, and a notch on that window only ties it: no new lead. But RS-039's "+7.7 points of crossings at equal alarms" for the notch was measured only on crossings the notch could score. 110 of 1,427 crossings (7.7%) have no notch score at all (a flank short of data, mostly next to gaps), and the old box score still finds about half of them. Over all crossings the notch's gain is about +2.9, not +7.7. That explains most of why the tier-level gain was only +2.1 to +2.9.**
