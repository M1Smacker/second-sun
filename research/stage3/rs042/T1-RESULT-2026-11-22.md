# RS-042 T1: TGLC-era QLP light is about 20% quieter on our stars (Claude, 22 Nov 2026, 11:16 pm)

**Plain line: found something useful. On our own design stars, QLP light from Sector 94 on (QLP switched to TESS-Gaia Light Curve photometry there) has about 0.80 times the white noise per hour of Sectors 56 to 93, while SPOC light over the same years does not improve (1.05). Leftover wiggles (red factor) do not improve (1.19 against SPOC's 1.12). Net search noise for QLP light: about 0.81 times. The gain is largest for faint stars (Tmag 12 to 13: 0.5 to 0.7 times the white noise). LIKELY: different stars sit in each era (111 stars with QLP 94+ rows, 262 rows), so star mix can bias it; the SPOC control removes the spacecraft-era effect only.**

Script: `t1_tglc_era.py` (own data: Fable X4 rows, white noise per hour and red factor of each planted crossing). Sources: QLP DRN 004 says TGLC has "better noise characteristics" for 10.5 < T < 13.5 and the team plans to reprocess all TESS data with TGLC (https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22236, CONFIRMED in the abstract and summary); TGLC paper (Han and Brandt 2023, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.03704); MAST TGLC HLSP covers stars to T = 16 for "all available sectors" (https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/tglc, page dated Dec 2024; current sector range NOT checked).

## What it means for P (estimate, UNCERTAIN)
QLP carries the most weak crossings (1,594 of 3,550 weak crossings in RS-039 A2, median Tmag 11.5). SNR on QLP light would rise about 1.2 to 1.25 times if every sector had TGLC photometry; at the faint end more. A rough guess is +2 to +4 points of P, the largest single lever left after D1, and it adds no new rule for false alarms to fight (it is better light, not a looser filter). **Availability (CONFIRMED, MAST TGLC page read 22 Nov 2026 11:14 pm, updated there 7 Dec 2025):** TGLC light curves for Sectors 1 to 55 (Sectors 12 to 55 only through per-sector bulk curl scripts, one line per file, with per-sector target lists mapping Gaia DR3 id to TIC id); about 0.8 MB per file (Sector 53: 4.25 million files, 3.5 TB). Sectors 56 to 93 are NOT on MAST (the open-source `tglc` package can make them from FFI cutouts; slow); 94 on are already TGLC inside QLP. For our roughly 1,200 searched stars at a few sectors each before 56, a pick-our-lines download is about 5 to 10 GB (estimate). It needs: TGLC light for our stars' older sectors (MAST HLSP, or wait for QLP's reprocessing), a loader path and tests, and a bench. Not started; a new design for Ahmed's go.
