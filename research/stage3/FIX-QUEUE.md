70. **M. A QLP-only star-sector's events depend on which other stars are in the data manifest (CONFIRMED 2 Nov 2026).** A QLP star-sector with no SPOC file of its own borrows its good-time windows from neighbouring stars of the same sector, camera and CCD (`stage2_search.py`, `neighbour_windows`), read from the run's manifest. The manifest grew after the 30 Oct sealed download, and 77 of 583 such groups (13%) in the development slice gave different events in the 2 Nov run than in the 29 Oct run; groups with their own SPOC windows never changed (2,651 of 2,651 identical), and SPOC and TESS-SPOC events were identical in every group. Effects: (1) an old run is not a like-for-like baseline once the manifest has changed: the 0.9 study therefore reran its 0.8.0 baseline under the same manifest (`dev_428c5fb454cd`, `rows_v9base.pkl`); (2) after the G3 download, rerunning G1 or G2 would change QLP-only events again; the manifest hash is stored in each run's settings but is not part of the Vesper version. Options: freeze the neighbour-window table per star list as a hashed file, or take neighbours only from the star's own list. Decide before the G3 preregistration (T112). About 0.5 point. Found by the offline comparison of the two development runs before the 0.9 accept rules were applied (the plan's "dev events under 0.8.0 equal the 0.7.1 events plus load-time c6" was UNVERIFIED and is now shown false for 13% of QLP-only groups).
