# T35: the 3-hour box, report-only split and its planet price (Claude, 14 Nov 2026; written before running)

**Question:** how much of the tier-1 background and how much of the planet bench sit in the shortest search box (3 h), under the adopted set B? Circumbinary transits are usually longer (TOI-1338 b predicted 4.7 to 13.5 h), so the 3 h box may catch mostly noise.
**Part 1 (report only, no search change):** untouched design-light background, primary SNR 7+, tier 1 under B, real and upside-down, split by box duration and binary-period bin (G2 and G3).
**Part 2 (bench price):** option B3 = B, but an event found in a box of 3 h or shorter is held at tier 2. Same harness, trials and meters as RS-029. Reported: P, paired control per bin, background real / upside-down, T156 (not run here).
**Not a decision:** B3 is not adopted by this file whatever it shows. Expected (written first): B3 removes most upside-down background (31 Oct count: 44 of 59 in 3 h) but also costs planets whose dips were caught by the 3 h box (short transits of fast, small binaries, and partial catches). If P falls, the trade goes to Fable's task 2 and Ahmed.

- Under B the 3 h box holds 57% of real and 51% of upside-down tier-1 events (31 Oct, before OAE and the other fixes: 62% and 75%). It no longer collects the upside-down alarms disproportionately.
- It holds most of the real EXCESS: 99 - 40 = 59 of the total 174 - 79 = 95. Boxes of 8 h and longer are near balance (29 real / 27 upside-down). LIKELY reading: short boxes catch eclipse leftovers and dump artefacts (one-sided, invisible to the upside-down meter), long boxes catch noise symmetric in both arms. NOT TESTED as a cause.
- Per binary-period bin the 3 h excess is spread (G2 14/5, 4/4, 22/6, 2/5, 21/9).

### Part 2 (14 Nov 2026, 11:29 pm; `rs032_box_bench.py`, `box.json`). CONFIRMED on the design bench (one key)
| Option | Dips new | Bumps new | P | Paired control | Background real / upside-down |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B (identity) | 525 | 513 | 0.4811 | passes in all 5 bins | 174 / 79 |
| B3 (3 h box held) | 485 | 467 | 0.4463 | **fails in 20 to 27.4 d** (93 dips / 77 bumps) | 75 / 39 |

- **B3 costs 40 planets (P 48.1% to 44.6%)**, mostly in fast binaries: dips per bin 99 to 81 (5 to 10 d), 102 to 86 (10 to 14 d), 90 to 87, 96 to 93, 138 to 138. Short transits of fast binaries are caught by the 3 h box; slow-binary planets are not.
- It halves the background (174 / 79 to 75 / 39) and cuts the real excess from 95 to 36.
- **Verdict: B3 FAILS rule 1** (P falls; paired control fails in one bin). The 3 h box stays. T35 decided: keep it.
- What it teaches: the 3 h box is where most of the unexplained real excess lives AND where fast-binary planets are found. A cut by box length cannot separate them; a cut by cause (eclipse leftovers, dump artefacts) is needed. This supports the current road (FIX 94 eclipse timing, dump-artefact work) rather than a box rule.
