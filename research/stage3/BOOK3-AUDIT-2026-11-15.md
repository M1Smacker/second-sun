## 1. Executive summary

**32 confirmed problems: 1 P0, 4 P1, 6 P2, 14 P3, 7 P4.** 28 came in with the Book 3 redesign (15 Nov, 3:49 to 7:08 am), 2 were already there (plus the older parts of M-16), and 2 have an unclear origin (the 404 page, and the archive packaging).

The book reads well on its ordinary pages: every page fits the screen except one (Claims at 320 px), with no script errors and no broken internal links. The failures sit almost entirely in the three things built overnight: the Tree home page, the guided tour and Twinlight.

- **You can get stuck.** On a phone the menu opens nothing on 8 pages: the Tree, Twinlight home and all 6 lessons. The Tree page has no other link out.
- **Light mode breaks the header on every page.** The title, menu, theme switch, search icon, Back button and PC tabs fall to contrast 1.1 to 1 (readable text needs 4.5 to 1).
- **The Tree on a phone overlaps itself.** Its toolbar stays pinned with no background and prints over the branch cards.
- **The first lesson can't be finished on an iPhone.** Three of its answers need a minus sign that the iPhone number pad doesn't have.

**Ready for use: no.** It is usable on a PC in dark mode. On a phone, the device you actually use, it is not usable until the P0 and P1 items are fixed.

## 2. Critical findings (P0 and P1)

### M-01 (P0) The menu opens nothing on the Tree, Twinlight home and all 6 lessons; on a phone the Tree has no way out. Found by: Both. VERIFIED. Your Bug 2.
- **Where:** `/`, `/learn/` and the 6 lessons, at every phone and tablet size (320 to 768 px wide, and 844x390 landscape), in both themes. On PC the top tabs still work.
- **Steps:** open the Tree on a phone and tap the three lines at the top left.
- **Expected:** the side menu slides in.
- **Actual:** the page dims slightly and nothing appears. The menu's switch flips on, but the menu panel is hidden.
  - The Tree page offers no link to any other page (measured: its only visible link points to itself).
  - Twinlight links only to its own lessons and the old /v2/ learning page.
  - The tour's last step tells phone users to "Open the menu at the top left", which cannot work.
- **Cause:** the front matter `hide: [navigation, toc, footer]` in `book/docs/index.md:2`, `book/docs/learn/index.md:2` and line 2 of every lesson. Material hides the side navigation everywhere, including inside the phone menu, but leaves the menu button showing.
- **Origin:** introduced by the redesign: a46ec2dd (Tree) and e8342b16 (Twinlight). In Book 2 (/v2/) the menu works on every page tested.
- **Evidence:** both audits' sweeps show 90 of 90 menu taps failing on these 8 pages (14 viewport and theme cells each); my menu tap at 390 px on the live Tree.
- **Confidence:** high.

### M-02 (P1) Light mode makes the header unreadable on every page. Found by: Both. VERIFIED. Your Bug 3.
- **Where:** all 47 normal pages, at every size.
  - Phone: the site title, menu icon, Back button, theme switch and search icon.
  - PC: also the Tree / Learning / Shelf tabs.
- **Steps:** tap the sun icon, then look at the top bar.
- **Expected:** dark text and icons on the light bar.
- **Actual:** colour #e6e8ee on about #f5f7fb. Contrast is 1.14 to 1 for the title and Back button, and 1.11 to 1 for the tabs. Headings inside the pages passed in both themes (none below 4.5 to 1).
- **Cause:** `book/docs/assets/book3.css:29` gives the header a background mixed from the page colour, which is near-white in light mode. The header's text colour is still `--md-primary-bg-color: #e6e8ee` from `extra.css:18`, which was chosen for the old dark header. Codex also points at `book3.css:30,32` (the tabs).
- **Origin:** introduced by the redesign, c56e08a1. Book 2's header was dark navy in both themes.
- **Evidence:** screenshots light-tree-390.png and light-tree-1280.png; the sweep (47 pages); Codex contrast.json.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Note:** if the heading you saw was something other than the top bar, please send a screenshot. My check found no in-page heading below 4.5 to 1.

### M-03 (P1) On a phone, the Tree's toolbar stays pinned with no background and prints over the branch cards. Found by: Claude. VERIFIED. Probably part of your Bug 1.
- **Where:** `/` at all phone sizes. In landscape (844x390) the header and this toolbar together take 181 of 390 px of height.
- **Steps:** open the Tree on a phone and scroll down.
- **Actual:**

  - Branch text shows through and collides with them.

- **Cause:** `book/docs/assets/tree.css:12`: `.sst-bar { position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 5 }` has no background, and `top: 0` ignores the 53 px header.
- **Origin:** introduced by the redesign, a46ec2dd.
- **Evidence:** home-scrolled-390.png.
- **Confidence:** high.

### M-04 (P1) The tour card stays fixed while the page scrolls, and its highlight ring stays stuck where its target used to be. Found by: Claude (Codex judged the fixed card "by design"; see section 8). VERIFIED. Your Bug 1.
- **Where:** `/` on the first open (or after "Show the tour again"), at every size.
- **Steps:** open the Tree for the first time and scroll before pressing Skip.
- **Actual:**
  - The "1 of 4 ... Skip / Next" card is fixed at the bottom and covers 211 of 844 px.
  - The page scrolls freely underneath (the tour is not modal).
  - On steps 2 and 3, the highlight ring has a 9999 px shadow that dims the whole page. It is drawn once at screen coordinates, so it stays put while its target scrolls away. Measured: ring at 180 px, target moved to -421 px.
  - On a phone, step 4 has no target and points to the dead menu (M-01).
- **Cause:**
  - `tree.css:159` (card `position: fixed`).
  - `tree.css:169` (ring `position: fixed` plus the huge shadow).
  - `tour.js:39-46` (ring placed once, never moved).
  - `tour.js:9` skips the tour in every automated test browser, so no test ever saw it.
- **Origin:** introduced by the redesign, 297e5d5b.
- **Evidence:** tour-step2-scrolled-390.png; the JSON from inter1.
- **Confidence:** high.

### M-05 (P1) Lesson 1.1 can't be finished on an iPhone: three answers need a minus sign. Found by: Claude. Code VERIFIED; iPhone keypad NOT TESTED on a device (backed by sources).
- **Where:** `learn/u1/l1-a-light-curve.md:64, 92, 102`: the answers are -35.8, -1 and -3.
- **Actual:**
  - When all of a problem's answers are numbers, the answer box sets `inputmode="decimal"` (`learn.js`, `problems()`).
  - On an iPhone that opens the decimal pad, which has no minus key ([WebKit bug 193825](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=193825), [CSS-Tricks on inputmode](https://css-tricks.com/?p=287475)).
  - The hint even says "the change is negative", but you cannot type it. A Unicode minus "−" pasted in is also rejected (VERIFIED).
- **Origin:** introduced by the redesign, e8342b16.
- **Confidence:** high in the code; likely on the device.

## 10. Recommended repair order (recommendations only)
1. **M-01, the menu.** It traps you, and it is the cheapest fix (front matter plus a CSS rule so the phone menu keeps the nav). Without it nothing else on the Tree or in Twinlight is reachable.
2. **M-02, the light header.** One CSS pairing, and it affects every page.
3. **M-03 and M-04, the Tree on a phone.** Give the toolbar a background and a top offset (or stop it being sticky). Make the tour modal (lock the scroll, move the ring or drop it) and fix M-06 and M-23 in the same pass.
4. **M-05, M-09, M-08, M-22, the answer checker.** Use a text keyboard (or offer a minus key), and accept comma decimals, units and the Unicode minus. Lesson 1.1 is the first thing you will do, so this sits next to the navigation fixes.
5. **M-07, notes.** Your notes must not vanish.
6. **M-10 and M-11.** The Claims overflow at 320, and modal focus plus scroll lock.
7. **The P3 Tree and Twinlight polish:** M-12 to M-21, M-24.
8. **The P4 items.**
9. **Before any fix is published:** add these checks to the publish gate so they cannot come back:
   - a menu-tap test on every page;
   - a contrast test in both themes;
   - an overflow test against the phone width (not the browser window);
   - the tour enabled in at least one test;
   - one recorded walk-through at 390 px in each theme.

## 11. Final verdict
Stability: good. No crashes, no script errors, no broken links. Usability on a phone: poor. You can be trapped on the home page, light mode hides the header, the Tree overlaps itself when scrolled, and the first lesson can't be finished. Readiness: not ready. The P0 and P1 fixes are small in code (mostly CSS and front matter). They need a phone walk-through in both themes before the book is called done again, ideally on your iPhone, because nothing in this audit ran on real Safari.
