## Why the bar moves
The hunt is the main road left, and G1 is one-shot. At P near 0.50, half of the planets we are built to find would be missed in the one run we get. Ahmed judged that too low.

## The number (unchanged definition)
P = planet-level recovery exactly as defined in `G1-PLANET-BAR.md`: reference planets (3,000 to 6,000 ppm, period 4 to 8 times the binary period, circumbinary orbit model, raw-light injection, 1,098 planets, equal weight per binary-period bin), 95% interval by star-cluster bootstrap. A multi-crossing method counts a planet as found when the method flags it at its own frozen tier-1 rule, measured with its own upside-down control.

2. **P from 0.20 to under 0.75, or the lower bound under 0.65: FIX FIRST.**
3. **P under 0.20: CHAIN BROKEN.**
Unchanged: any binary-period bin under 0.20 is a named blind spot; the upside-down control must pass the 004 conditions for whatever method is used; the winning combination must be confirmed on a fresh planet bench (new seed) it was not chosen on.
