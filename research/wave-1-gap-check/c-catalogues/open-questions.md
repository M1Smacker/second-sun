# Wave 1c open questions

Ranked by how much the answer could change the hunt.

1. **Is TESS funded past FY2026?** The FY2027 request cuts Astrophysics ~65% (to ~$552M) and the House bill rejects the cuts, but I found no source that names TESS's FY27 line. The GI office still publishes planned pointings to Sector 134 (Sep 2028). Next step: NASA FY27 congressional justification, Astrophysics "Operating Missions" table (the PDF's text could not be extracted with stdlib tools); or the AAS FY27 post (blocks automated fetches).
2. **Has anyone extended the TESS Ten Thousand search past Sector 82, or vetted part of its ~873k unvetted list?** 1b found an Oddo talk "searching the TESS 10k sample" and the Tufts STANLEY GI programme. An unpublished S83+ EB list from the GSFC / Eclipsing Binary Patrol team is plausible. A polite question to Kostov's group would settle it (Claude drafts, Ahmed sends), only if it saves months.
3. **What is the latest sector each light-curve product has actually delivered?** QLP's MAST page has a "Year 8 (Sectors 97-107)" table; TESS-SPOC lists through Year 7 (S84-96); TGLC's page lists only Years 1-5 (S1-65). For TOI-1338, CAOM shows TESS-SPOC only through S69 and TGLC only S1-11. Wave 2 should query CAOM per product per sector so we know which faint EBs need DIY photometry from TESScut.
4. **Which stars got the longest continuous stare in the EM3 block (S97-107)?** My 600-star sample found at most 4 of those 11 sectors per star (C40), so the "rolled" design gives longer-than-usual baselines, not months on end, for a random catalogued EB. A full map (all 10,001 TTT EBs through TESScut, ~3 hours at polite speed, or tess-point once installed) would find the stars with the longest new baselines, which are the best places to hunt.
5. **Villanova live TESS EB catalogue:** only page 1 was read. Its per-target sector lists run past S26 (e.g. S88), but is its membership still the 2022 set? Count all ~46 pages, or ask.
6. **IJspeert et al. 2024 exact sector range** ("first 4 years", likely S1-55) and why VizieR has 69,057 candidate rows vs 14,573 "well characterised": which column marks the clean sample?
7. **Where are the Oddo et al. 2025 (1,292 M+M EBs) and Liu et al. 2026 (20,196 classified EBs) tables?** Not on VizieR yet (as of today's tap_schema query). Probably journal machine-readable tables.
8. **TOI-1338 b in Sectors 97-98 (Sep 2025 to Jan 2026):** 1a says a transit has a ~30% chance per conjunction after 2025-09-12. Was one caught? This is a perfect first hands-on test (data exist at 2-min and 20-s). Do not answer from memory; predict with the published ephemeris and look.
9. **TIC 319011894 (Davies+ 2026 single-event candidate) was re-observed in Sector 87 (FFI, 200 s).** Nobody has published on that sector for it. Worth a look in wave 3/4 as a practice target.
10. **How many of TTT's 872,720 unvetted candidates are Gaia DR3 eclipsing binaries?** A VizieR TAP cross-match (I/358/veb, 2.18M rows) could turn the unvetted list into a few thousand "Gaia-confirmed" EBs nobody has searched. Doable later with one TAP job; not done here.
