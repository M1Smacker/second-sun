## Part A: the learning track

| Finding | Source | Label | What it says for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Of ten study techniques, only practice testing and distributed practice were rated high; rereading, highlighting and summarising were rated low; interleaved practice moderate | Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan, Willingham 2013, Psychological Science in the Public Interest 14(1), 4-58, [APS summary](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade.html) | LIKELY | Reading chapters is the weak part of the track. Questions and spacing are the strong part. |
| Perceptual learning modules (many short classified trials, varied displays, feedback after every trial) improved pattern recognition in medicine, mathematics and aviation, with gains that lasted a month in one study | Kellman and colleagues, [Kellman lab papers](https://kellmanlab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/357/2025/09/kellman_et_al_2008.pdf) | LIKELY, but mostly from one lab; effect sizes not read | The rehearsal is the training for the eye, not a test of it. The track gives words; the trials give the recognition. |
| Learners who study mixed examples do better on new items yet feel that blocked study helped more | Kornell and Bjork 2008; replicated by Verkoeijen and Bouwmeester 2014 and with older adults ([search summary](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.770885/full)) | LIKELY | Feeling bad at a mixed test is what the method feels like. Do not make the scored test blocked to feel better. |
| People correct confident errors more than unsure ones once they see the answer | Butterfield and Metcalfe 2001, replicated many times ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3079415)) | LIKELY for the effect, UNCERTAIN for why | The confidence rating is worth keeping. The feedback after a sure-but-wrong answer should be the most explicit one. |
| Guidance that helps novices can hurt experts; worked examples first, then faded | Kalyuga, expertise reversal effect ([overview](https://openlearning.mit.edu/mit-faculty/research-based-learning-findings/worked-and-faded-examples)) | LIKELY; how "expertise" is measured is questioned | Ahmed is a novice at this task: more guidance now, less later. |
| Planet Hunters TESS shows volunteers simulated planets: 30% of the first 10 classifications, falling to 1% by 100; the volunteer is told the answer each time; only simulations of clear size are shown | Eisner and colleagues, [Planet Hunters TESS II, arXiv 2011.13944](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13944) | LIKELY (read through a search summary); no per-volunteer accuracy found | A working precedent for a training ramp: many clear known fakes with feedback at first, tapering. |
| Mastery learning (pass a check before the next step) gave about 0.5 standard deviations on average; the famous 2-sigma claim is not supported | Kulik, Kulik, Bangert-Drowns 1990; von Hippel on Bloom ([Education Next](https://www.educationnext.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/ednext_XIV_2_von_hippel.pdf)) | LIKELY | A level gate helps a little. It is not a reason to force Ahmed through the whole track before the test. |
| Deliberate practice explained 26% of variance in games, 21% in music, 18% in sport, 4% in education | Macnamara, Hambrick, Oswald 2014; disputed by Ericsson and colleagues on how practice was measured ([PsychSci PDF](https://www.psychologie.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:658d39fe-2f4b-467f-9ae2-ffeb25caf2b6/McNamara.PsychSci.25.2014.pdf)) | UNCERTAIN | Practice helps, hours alone do not guarantee skill. Keep the pass line on measured results, never on hours. |

Limit that applies to all of them: nearly every study is on students in class or lab tasks. Whether the size of the effect carries over to an adult learning light-curve classification alone is UNCERTAIN.

## Part B: knowing we are on the right path without experts

The plain answer: reading more is the weakest check. A stronger check is any run that could have failed and whose answer does not come from us.

| Check | Source | Label | In Second Sun |
|---|---|---|---|
| Known-answer tests (a planet we already know, planted dips) | standard practice; PHT uses injected signals the same way | LIKELY | Done: planted dips, TOI-1338 b (7 of 10 passes). |
| Negative controls: run the same method where the answer must be nothing; a signal there means a fault; a quiet result does not prove the method is clean | Lipsitch, Tchetgen Tchetgen, Cohen 2010, Epidemiology 21(3), 383-388 ([PDF](https://www.meb.ki.se/biostat/Sing2022/papers/Session6/Lipsitch%20EPI%202010%20neg%20controls%20for%20detecting%20confounding%20and%20bias.pdf)) | LIKELY | Done in one form: the upside-down control. More forms are open (shuffled time, shifted windows, wrong-star windows). |
| Blind analysis: fix every method choice before seeing the result | MacCoun and Perlmutter, Nature 526, 187-189 (2015) ([PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/526187a.pdf)) | LIKELY | Done: preregistration, sealed split, blind pictures. It guards against our bias, not against a wrong model. |
| Keep a score of our own predictions | forecasting literature | UNCERTAIN (see T116) | Partly: prereg predictions; Ahmed's confidence ratings. |
| Reproduce a published number from raw data (a period, a depth in a paper's table) | my own reasoning, no source searched | NOT TESTED | TOI-1338 b covers some of it. A Kepler circumbinary planet would add a second. |
| Ask a model to review the work | Huang and colleagues 2023, [arXiv 2310.01798](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798v1): models struggle to self-correct reasoning without external feedback; Panickssery, Bowman, Feng, NeurIPS 2024, [arXiv 2404.13076](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.13076v1): models favour their own outputs | LIKELY | My own review, and Compass passes by the same model family, are the weakest evidence we have. A script that can contradict me is worth more. |
| Feynman 1974 "Cargo Cult Science", Caltech Engineering and Science 37(7): on not fooling yourself and reporting what could make the result wrong | [Caltech](https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm) | Existence CONFIRMED by the search; the exact sentence was not seen, UNCERTAIN | Already our rule: a clean measured null counts as a win. |
