# Second Sun: standing rules (read with RESUME.md)

## Verify before concluding (Ahmed, 28 Oct 2026; applies to the whole project, every session)
**Never leave a scientific doubt or uncertain finding unverified when reliable public sources are available.**
- When unsure about a scientific claim, astronomical concept, dataset, pipeline behaviour, detection result or methodological choice, investigate it before drawing conclusions. Use public sources as fits the question: NASA documentation and the NASA Exoplanet Archive, TESS and MAST archives, ADS and arXiv papers, official mission guides, scientific wikis, other credible sources.
- Prefer primary sources, official documentation, peer-reviewed work and original datasets over informal summaries. Cross-check important claims against more than one independent source when practical. A search-engine summary is a lead, not a source: open the paper or the table before relying on it.
- Label every important finding **CONFIRMED, LIKELY, UNCERTAIN or NOT TESTED**, with links. Explain conflicting evidence. Say plainly when reliable evidence is unavailable.
- Never invent facts, citations, verification or certainty. Never mistake an assumption for an established fact. Never imply a check was done when it was not.
- Cheapest check first: our own data and code (a script that can contradict me) before the web; the web before a guess. Keep it proportionate to the usage cap: verify what a conclusion or a published sentence rests on, and record what stays UNCERTAIN in the relevant notes file.

## How to research (Ahmed, 29 Oct 2026)
Every research task follows `research/RESEARCH-BLUEPRINT.md`: questions tied to decisions, our own data and the precision of our measures first, then primary sources (arXiv abstract and HTML pages, never file links in the browser), the exact sentence pulled out, labels, and every finding turned into a plan change and filed. Spend at least about 10 minutes on a serious question. It applies automatically, without being told (Ahmed, 3 Nov 2026): no fixed number of questions, only the research stint's own usage cap written down before it starts; every stint is numbered (RS-001 on) in `research/research-sessions.md`.
