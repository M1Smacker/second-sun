**Plain:** TESS's pointing wobbles more and more before each momentum dump, spilling starlight out of the aperture: a fake dip that our upside-down check can never see. A version of the dump-shape rule that only relaxes after the dump is fair but gains only 4 planets, so it is not the route to 0.50.
**Why it matters:** explains the blind spot Codex found, and moves Fable's attention to the eclipse-timing fix, the bigger lever.
**Explanation:** Four papers agree: scatter grows for about a day before a dump (Vanderburg 2019), pointing stays disturbed for hours after (Eisner 2019), with ramps within half a day (Wong 2020), and dumps mimic transits (Guerrero 2021). The planet gains of the shape rule sit in the same place as its fake dips, so only a direct wobble measure could separate them.
**Technical:** B 525/513 P 0.4811 bg 174/79; B+DS 545/531 P 0.4998 bg 204/83; B+DSp 529/519 P 0.4847 bg 176/80 (fails by 1 upside-down).
