**Plain:** The research on how people learn best says: a fully worked example first, then one half done, then problems you answer yourself with quick, specific feedback; start from the real thing and build the idea up.
**Why it matters:** Every Twinlight lesson follows that order, and the units start from a light curve on screen instead of from theory.
