**Plain:** The shortest search box (3 hours) stays: dropping it would cut false alarms in half but lose 40 test planets.
**Why it matters:** Most of the unexplained real dips and most fast-binary planets are found in the same box, so box length cannot sort them; the cause has to.
**Explanation:** Planets around fast binaries cross their stars quickly, so their dips are short. The same short box also picks up eclipse leftovers and spacecraft artefacts.
**Technical:** Under B the 3 h box holds 99/40 of 174/79 tier-1 background. Holding it (B3): P 0.4811 to 0.4463, background 75/39, paired control fails in 20 to 27.4 d (93/77). Rule written before the run.
