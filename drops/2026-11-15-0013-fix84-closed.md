**Plain:** A feared gap in our bad-data filter for newer QLP light curves does not exist: every flagged moment is already removed.
**Why it matters:** About 10% of newer QLP data are flagged bad; if they had slipped through, they could have made fake dips in G3 and future searches.
**Explanation:** QLP changed which bit marks bad exposures from sector 56. We feared our loader only knew the old bit. Counting on our own files shows the loading library uses the new bit for QLP files.
**Technical:** 4,403 QLP files (S56+), 9.6% of cadences flagged with bit 2^30; 0 of 61,289 flagged cadences in 60 loaded files survive detect_v2.load. Closed, no code change.
