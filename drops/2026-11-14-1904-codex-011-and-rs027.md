**Plain:** The narrower eclipse masks fail: they stay just under 50% and add a few false alarms, because the wide padding was hiding eclipses whose catalogue times are wrong.
**Why it matters:** the fix for FIX 94 must measure where each binary's eclipses really are in TESS, period included, before narrowing anything.
**Explanation:** With the fairness fix on, both variants catch about 16 more planets (P about 49.6%). About half the extra events on real light are deep eclipses (up to 17% deep). One binary repeats a 10,000 ppm eclipse every 31.81 days; its catalogue says 30.98.
**Technical:** V0/V1/V2 + OAE: P 48.20 / 49.61 / 49.66%; all-design background 176/83, 209/86, 205/89; T156 dips 500, 524, 523; paired control passes every bin. 18 of 38 new V2 real events are 8,000 ppm+ or SNR 50+, on 10 stars.
