**Plain:** The wobble of the star's light centre in our own files cannot tell the telescope's fake dips near momentum dumps from real planets.
**Why it matters:** It rules out a cheap safety check for the dump-shape rule; the only independent check left needs TESS's 2-second pointing records.
**Explanation:** The fake dips come from fast pointing jitter. Our light curves average the light centre over 2 to 30 minutes, which smooths that jitter away. Events the shape rule would free wobble no more than planted planets do.
**Technical:** Method and pass written first. G2: 25 freed real events against 415 planted crossings in the dump zone, Mann-Whitney p 0.53 (needed under 0.01); a guard at the planets' 90th percentile (J 1.90) holds 4 of 25. G3: 3 events, holds 1 (needs 2). Exploratory drift measure: weak hint (p 0.05). First run had a sector-name lookup bug (s2019), fixed before reading results.
