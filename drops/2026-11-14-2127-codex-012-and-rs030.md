**Plain:** Codex tried to measure each binary's true eclipse times from TESS itself; its own checks failed, mostly because the method sometimes mistakes a binary with two equal eclipses for one with half the period.
**Why it matters:** the eclipse-mask fix (the biggest fixable planet loss) needs these times; the problem is now narrow and nameable, so Fable's Monday round can fix it.
**Explanation:** On control stars whose timing we trust, 13 of 58 fits landed on half the true period and none of those agreed; on the correct period, 38 of 45 agreed within about a minute and a half (0.001 d). The eclipses that the narrower mask let through do line up with Codex's flagged tracks (18 of 21), which supports the earlier finding that the catalogue's times are wrong for those stars.
**Technical:** control agreement 28/60 periods, 31/60 epochs (54 needed); injection robust on all 9 testable stars (max shift 0.039 widths), 11 untestable. Fence check: frozen files 0, fence ids 0; branch not merged.
