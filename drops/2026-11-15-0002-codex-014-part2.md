**Plain:** Our own eclipse timing now gets the binaries' periods right, but it underestimates its own errors, so its quality check throws away most good results.
**Why it matters:** The eclipse-timing fix is the main road to the 50% planet bar; it is closer than the FAIL suggests, and the remaining fix is known.
**Explanation:** Codex fixed the half-period trap (all test binaries right). The new fits match the catalogue periods for 50 of 58 control stars within about a minute and a half. But each eclipse time is quoted as about twice as precise as it really is, so ordinary scatter looks like a timing problem and the track is discarded.
**Technical:** Preregistered checks failed (3-sigma period 15/60 seed 12, 11/40 hold-out). 39 of 58 tracks large-residual flagged; 33 of them within 0.001 d of the catalogue. Reduced chi-square 4.69 after per-product scaling. Seed 14 hold-out now spent.
