**Plain:** About a third of our top-tier events on real light are deeper than any planet could make; they are almost surely eclipses.
**Why it matters:** a guard that drops them would cut false work in G1, but our test bench cannot yet show it never drops a planet.
**Explanation:** 51 of 176 real and 16 of 83 upside-down top-tier events are over 3% deep. The bench's planted planets stop at 0.6% deep, so it cannot price the guard. Better than a fixed number: a ceiling per star from its size.
**Technical:** thresholds 10k/20k/30k/50k ppm: real 75/56/51/39, upside-down 35/19/16/7; on V2's 38 new real events only 7 over 30k.
