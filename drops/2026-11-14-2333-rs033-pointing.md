**Plain:** TESS's own 2-second pointing record shows the telescope shakes hard at each momentum dump but stays calm before it, so the shaking does not explain the extra fake-looking dips we see hours before dumps.
**Why it matters:** It saves a 46 GB download and tells Fable the pre-dump excess needs another explanation.
**Explanation:** Our light curves smooth out fast shaking, so we read the spacecraft's pointing directly for one sector. The fast shake jumps 4.5 times at the dump and stays a little high for hours after. Before the dump the pointing only drifts slowly by a tiny amount.
**Technical:** Sector 11, 4 cameras, 15 dumps. 30-min RMS ratio: 0.95 at 12 to 6 h before, up to 1.06 in the last hour, 4.5 at the dump, 1.13 to 1.28 after. Slow drift 0.3 to 0.6 arcsec (about 0.02 to 0.03 pixel), smooth. One sector only (UNCERTAIN for other eras).
