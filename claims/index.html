    Claims register¶

    In plain words

    Ahmed, every fact the book relies on lives here once, with how sure we are and where it came from. Pages show a small label next to a fact; tap it and you land on its row here.

    How to read the labels¶

    The shape carries the meaning, not the colour. VERIFIED (solid outline) means we read the original source or measured it ourselves. LIKELY (dashed) means a summary or secondary source. UNVERIFIED (dotted) means memory or one weak source: test before relying on it.
