    How to read this book¶

    Start here · about 5 min

    In plain words

    Read the chapters in order, and do each Part's labs on your PC when you reach them. Chapters work on your phone; labs need the PC. Every fact has a small label showing how sure we are, and you can tap it to see the source.

    The six Parts¶

    Part Question it answers Chapters Labs
    1. The sky and the data What are we looking at, and where does it come from? 1 to 3 1, 2, 3
    2. Two suns Why are two-star planets special, and hard to find? 4 to 6 5
    3. Finding dips How do we find a planet's dip in noisy data? 7 to 9 4, 6
    4. Is it real? How do we tell a planet from an impostor? 10, 11 7
    5. The professionals' tool What does STANLEY do that we don't? 12 8
    6. How the project runs Our workshop, credit, our mistakes 13 to 15 9

    The menu lists every page; all of them are finished. Later additions (more chapters, more labs) will say so in a field note.

    Inside a chapter¶

    Every chapter has the same seven steps, so you always know where you are:

    1. The intuition: an everyday picture.
    2. The scientific meaning: what scientists mean by it.
    3. The technical explanation: the numbers and formulas.
    4. Our implementation: how our code does it.
    5. Worked example: on our own data.
    6. What this lets us conclude, and what it doesn't.
    7. Check your understanding: tap a question to see the answer.

    The labels¶

    Every fact in the book carries one of three evidence labels. They are the ones the claims register allows, and the page will not build with any other:

    Label Meaning
    VERIFIED Read in the original source, or measured by us on your PC
    LIKELY From a summary or a paper's abstract, consistent with other sources
    UNVERIFIED From memory or one weak source: never used to make a decision

    How far a test has got is written in plain words inside sentences: "not tested yet", "still uncertain", "blocked". Those are not labels. (In my reports to you, "CONFIRMED" means the same as VERIFIED.)

    Tap any label to open the claims register, which lists every fact, its source and the date it was checked.

    The readiness bar¶

    The home page shows Mission readiness, 0 to 100: how complete and how well built our plan is. It is not the chance of finding a planet. Every point has proof, some points are counted by the program, and the number can go down. The full list, with the proof for each point, is on the Progress page.

    Labs¶

    Labs are where you do the work yourself. Each one has commands to copy, the output you should see (from a real run on your PC), what can go wrong, and a Your turn box. When you finish one, tell me "Lab 2 done" plus your result, and I'll check it.

    Field notes¶

    After every work session, a dated lesson goes into the Field notes. That's the rule from 20 October: nothing we do stays only in the chat.

    Suggested order¶

    1. The story so far (12 min)
    2. Lab 0, at your PC (10 min)
    3. Chapter 1, then Lab 1 and Lab 2
    4. Then Chapters 2 and 3, and on through the Parts.
