    The journey path¶

    Start here · 15 stops · in the order the project needed them

    In plain words

    The same fifteen chapters, but in the order we met each idea while doing the work. Each stop says what happened, and some link to the field note of that day.

    1. Chapter 4: Two suns19 Oct: we chose the hunt. Planets that orbit two stars are rare: only 14 are known to cross. VERIFIED Field note
    2. Chapter 5: Wandering transits19 Oct: we saw why the usual tools miss them. Their dips arrive off the clock, and that is our opening. Field note
    3. Chapter 1: Stars, names and missions19 Oct: we chose G1, 549 Kepler two-star systems, and matched Kepler's names to TESS's. Field note
    4. Chapter 2: Light curves19 Oct: every search starts from a light curve, brightness against time, so this is the skill we use most.
    5. Chapter 3: Our data19 Oct: we measured how big TESS files are on your PC and where they come from. Field note
    6. Chapter 6: Orbits and the three-body problem20 Oct: the Bible Overhaul wrote down the orbit maths behind the wandering.
    7. Chapter 7: Noise and statistics19 to 20 Oct: the overnight run showed that noise alone makes fake dips.
    8. Chapter 8: Our detector19 to 20 Oct: our detector re-found TOI-1338 b in 4 of 8 sectors, then we kept testing it. Field note
    9. Chapter 9: Fake planets20 Oct: hiding fake planets in real data showed us how small a planet we can see. Field note
    10. Chapter 12: STANLEY19 to 20 Oct: STANLEY, the professionals' tool, was installed and pointed near the right period.
    11. Chapter 10: Vetting19 to 20 Oct: our first G1 star, Kepler-16, flagged one dip. It was the telescope wobbling.
    12. Chapter 11: Pixels and neighbours20 Oct: we wrote down how to check that a dip is on our star. The pixel check in our pipeline is still to do.
    13. Chapter 13: Your workshop20 Oct: the Bible Overhaul wrote down where everything lives and the safety rules.
    14. Chapter 14: Credit and publishing20 Oct: NASA's candidate site now wants a paper first, so we mapped how credit works.
    15. Chapter 15: Our mistakes, as lessons20 to 21 Oct: fifteen logged mistakes became five patterns and a rule each.

    When you reach the end, the Hunt tab shows where the project stands now.
